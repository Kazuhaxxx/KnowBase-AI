# Laptop Docking Station, Battery & Hardware RMA Guide

**Document ID:** KB-HW-009  
**Organization:** NovaTech Global (Fictional Demo Knowledge Base)  
**Classification:** Employee  

## 1. USB-C / Thunderbolt Docking Station Not Detecting External Monitors
1. Verify the dock's dedicated **130W/180W AC power brick** is plugged into the wall outlet (bus-powered operation cannot drive dual 4K displays).
2. Unplug the Thunderbolt USB-C cable from the laptop, wait **15 seconds**, and reconnect it to the port marked with a **lightning bolt / Thunderbolt** icon.
3. Perform a **Dock Power Drain**: unplug power from the dock for 30 seconds, hold the dock power button for 10 seconds, then reconnect.
4. Press `Win + P` and ensure Windows display mode is set to **Extend** rather than **PC screen only**.

## 2. Laptop Battery Draining Rapidly or Not Charging ("Plugged In, Not Charging")
1. Confirm you are using the original OEM USB-C charger rated for **65W or higher** (phone chargers trickle-charge or fail under load).
2. Check **Power & battery** settings in Windows to verify **Smart Charging / Battery Health Manager** is not intentionally capping charge at 80% for longevity.
3. Perform an embedded controller (EC) reset: shut down completely, disconnect AC and all USB devices, and hold the power button for **20 seconds**, then reconnect power and boot.

## 3. Hardware Replacement & Loaner Laptop RMA Process
If a corporate laptop suffers screen damage, keyboard failure, or swollen battery:
**Important:** If a battery appears swollen or the chassis is warping, power off the device immediately, unplug AC power, and bring it to Workplace IT — do not ship swollen batteries by standard courier.
1. Log a **Hardware Repair / Loaner Request** ticket with your laptop's **Asset Tag** (sticker on bottom chassis, e.g., `NT-LPT-4821`).
2. Ensure your OneDrive Desktop/Documents backup shows **Up to date** before handoff.
3. Same-day hot-swap loaner laptops are issued at Building 1 & Building 3 IT Bars for Priority-1 hardware failures.
