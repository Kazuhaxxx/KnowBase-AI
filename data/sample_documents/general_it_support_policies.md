# NovaTech General IT Support & SLA Policy

**Document ID:** KB-POL-010  
**Organization:** NovaTech Global (Fictional Demo Knowledge Base)  
**Classification:** Public  

## 1. IT Service Desk Operating Hours & Channels
- **Self-Service AI Copilot & Knowledge Base:** Available 24/7.
- **Live IT Service Desk Chat & Phone (`ext. 4357`):** Monday–Friday, 07:00–19:00 local regional time.
- **24/7 Emergency P1 Escalation Line:** For major outages affecting >10 employees, production security incidents, or complete VPN gateway unavailability.

## 2. Ticket Priority & Target Response SLAs
- **Priority 1 (Critical / Outage):** Initial response within **15 minutes**, target resolution **4 hours**.
- **Priority 2 (High / Work-Blocking Individual):** Initial response within **2 hours**, target resolution **1 business day**.
- **Priority 3 (Medium / Standard Request):** Initial response within **4 business hours**, target resolution **3 business days**.
- **Priority 4 (Low / General Inquiry):** Initial response within **1 business day**, target resolution **5 business days**.

## 3. Acceptable Use & Data Handling Reminder
- Corporate devices and accounts must only be used in accordance with the NovaTech Information Security Policy.
- Never share your corporate password, MFA push approvals, or Temporary Access Passes with anyone — including IT staff. IT Support will never ask for your password.
- Remote screen-sharing sessions must only be initiated through the approved **NovaTech Remote Assist** portal after ticket verification.
