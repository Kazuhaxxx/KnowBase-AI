# NovaTech Global — Parental Leave & Family Support Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2026-04  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Department:** People Operations & Global HR  
**Access Classification:** Employee  
**Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope
NovaTech Global provides comprehensive parental leave benefits to support employees welcoming a new child through birth, adoption, or surrogacy. This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees working 20+ hours per week who have completed at least 90 days of continuous service.

---

## 2. Paid Parental Leave Entitlements

### 2.1 Primary Caregiver Leave
- **Duration:** Eligible primary caregivers receive **16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave**.
- **Eligibility:** Available to birth mothers, fathers designated as primary caregivers, adopting parents, and intended parents via surrogacy.
- **Utilization:** May be taken continuously or in two separate blocks within the first 12 months following the child's birth or legal placement.

### 2.2 Secondary Caregiver / Paternity Leave
- **Duration:** Eligible secondary caregivers (including fathers, non-birthing parents, and secondary adoptive parents) receive **8 weeks of 100% paid leave**.
- **Salary Continuation:** Leave is fully paid at 100% of the employee's regular base salary.
- **Timing:** Must be utilized within the first 12 months following birth or placement.

---

## 3. Gradual Return-to-Work Program
To ease transition back into workplace responsibilities:
1. Employees may work a **80% schedule at 100% full pay** for their first 4 weeks back from leave.
2. Managers must not assign overnight on-call rotations or mandatory weekend shifts during the gradual return period.

---

## 4. How to Request Leave
1. Submit an initial notice to People Operations via the **Workday HR Portal** at least **30 calendar days** prior to the anticipated start date.
2. Attach proof of expected birth date (medical certificate) or legal placement documentation (adoption agency certificate).
3. Contact the Benefits Team at `benefits@novatech-demo.internal` for assistance with salary continuation and health insurance continuation.
