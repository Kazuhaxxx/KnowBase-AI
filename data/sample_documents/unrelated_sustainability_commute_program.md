# Green Commute Bike & Transit Subsidy Program

**Document ID:** HR-BEN-014  
**Organization:** NovaTech Global (Fictional People Operations Notice)  
**Classification:** Public  

## Program Overview
NovaTech encourages low-emission commuting by offering a **$120/month transit and micro-mobility reimbursement** to full-time employees.

## Eligible Expenses
- Monthly metro, rail, bus, and ferry passes.
- Bike-share memberships and e-bike maintenance up to $300 annually.
- Secure badge-access bicycle lockers are available in Building 2 Garage Level B1.

## How to Claim
Upload your monthly transit receipt in the Benefits Portal by the 25th of each month for payroll reimbursement on the first pay cycle of the following month.
