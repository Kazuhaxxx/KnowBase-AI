# Campus Bistro Weekly Menu & Catering Schedule

**Document ID:** FAC-MENU-012  
**Organization:** NovaTech Global (Fictional Facilities Notice)  
**Classification:** Public  

## Weekly Lunch Specials (Building 1 & Building 3 Cafeteria)
- **Monday:** Herb-roasted chicken grain bowls, roasted cauliflower soup, and Mediterranean flatbread.
- **Tuesday:** Build-your-own taco bar with black beans, grilled fish, and cilantro lime rice.
- **Wednesday:** Teriyaki salmon bento, vegetable ramen station, and edamame salad.
- **Thursday:** Artisan pasta bar with pesto or marinara, garlic bread, and Caesar salad.
- **Friday:** Grass-fed burger grill, sweet potato fries, and seasonal fruit tarts.

## Catering Requests
Team lunch catering requests must be submitted at least 48 hours in advance via the Facilities Catering Form. Dietary allergies should be listed per attendee.
